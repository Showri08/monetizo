FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

Do you guarantee monetization approval?

Platforms make the final decision, so no one can honestly guarantee it. We prepare your account to meet every eligibility requirement and fix issues that commonly cause rejection.

Do you need my password?

No. We never ask for your password. We work through page roles, shared access or by guiding you step by step.

Is it safe to pay?

You pay by Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal or bank transfer. We never ask for card numbers, PINs or one-time codes. Never share these with anyone.

What if I'm not eligible yet?

We'll tell you what's missing and build a plan to get you there.
